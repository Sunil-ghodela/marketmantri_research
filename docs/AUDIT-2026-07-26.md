## 4. Analysis (interpretation)

**Strengths**
- Methodological maturity unusual for a solo 4-month project: walk-forward splits, OOS discipline, fee-aware nets, a rejected-hypotheses log (43 entries — most projects hide these), and above all the **self-caught look-ahead bug with a public Sharpe restatement** (6.08→2.998). That last item is the strongest credibility signal in the repo.
- The paper record is explicitly framed as a **"money-gate"** (~3 months forward before capital) — the correct control, already self-imposed.
- Traceability is good: nearly every claim in docs maps to a script + results artifact.

**Risks**
- **Evidence is backtest + ~1 month paper.** Nothing here supports capital deployment yet; the project's own money-gate says the same.
- **Sizing scheme naming/behaviour:** multipliers that *increase after losses* (O9) are martingale-family behaviour despite the "anti-martingale" label in the commit. Whatever the name, loss-escalation fattens drawdown tails; `_portfolio_crash_stress.py` exists but stress results were not reviewed here. Requires review before any real capital.
- **Astro features (O10):** as gated, data-tested features they are methodologically admissible (test-and-reject); as public-facing material they are a credibility liability. Keep internal.
- **Single-operator + AI-agent authorship (O12):** no second human reviewer; errors like the look-ahead bug were caught, but the process depends on one person's gating.
- **Data: Yahoo 1m with "Kite upgrade pending"** (O5 config) — known-weak data underlying the live paper run.
