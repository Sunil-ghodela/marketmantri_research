About

An independent, self-funded research effort from India — built at night, on real markets, with a bias for documented failure over undocumented success.

What this is

A public log of systematic-trading research on Indian markets (NSE) and, via independent platforms, US cross-sections. The stack is Python — vectorized backtesting harnesses, walk-forward replays, cost models, live paper engines on a VPS with cron-driven scans and full audit trails.

What this is not

Not a signal service. Not a fund. Not investment advice. No returns are promised, no money is managed, nothing is for sale. The value here is the process — and the process is checkable in public.

The one-line resume of the method

pre-register → train → sealed test → verdict → paper gate → only then a real conversation. 35 ideas died so far. Good.
