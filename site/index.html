§ 02

Four rules I don't break

  • The verdict is written before the test. Champion rules and pass/fail thresholds are committed to git before a single simulation runs. No moving goalposts.
  • One sealed look. The out-of-sample period is hidden by the tooling itself. It gets opened once, for the declared champion. Fail → closed forever, no post-test tweaks.
  • Costs on every trade, every time. A backtest without fees is fiction. I learned this the hard way — a strategy that won +21% over two years came out at +0.017% per trade across the full decade with costs counted, and negative in the current year. It's in the graveyard now.
  • Failures get published. The rejection log is the moat. 35 documented deaths protect the few things that survive.
§ 03

Why believe any of this?

Because it's checkable. The research log — including the pre-registration commits, the rejection ledger and the harness code that reproduces my headline claims — is public on GitHub. The live experiment page updates weekly with the paper record versus what the backtest predicted — including the weeks where they disagree.

The most impressive thing about a strategy is not its returns. It's whether the research process was designed to challenge it.
