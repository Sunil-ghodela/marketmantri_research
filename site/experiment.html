The live experiment ● running

Backtests are promises. Paper records are receipts. Two engines run unattended on a VPS; every trade is recorded fees-net with a full audit trail (each exit carries a snapshot of the signal that caused it). This page gets a new row every week — whatever the numbers say.

Experiment 1 — index engine (started 3 Aug 2026)

A 1-hour MACD-cross family engine on NIFTY (exact parameters private). One shadow record produces three books simultaneously:

Experiment 2 — stock basket (fresh record since 28 Jul 2026)

A 15-slot long-only momentum basket over a ~90-name NSE universe. Its 10-year backtest is positive every single year fees-net — but the honest version of that claim is "the recent-year edge is about half the golden-era edge." The paper record exists to find out which year 2026 wants to be. Checkpoint: 29 Aug 2026 (30 days of clean record).
